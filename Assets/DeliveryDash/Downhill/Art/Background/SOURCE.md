# Downhill background assets

Researched and downloaded from the official publishers on 2026-10-05.

- [Kenney City Kit (Suburban)](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban), version 2.0, CC0. Selected original OBJ building types a, h, p and t, plus the original color palette texture. OBJ files retain their content and are stored with a `.txt` extension for Unity runtime mesh loading. The original bundled license is `city-LICENSE.txt`.
- [Kenney Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit), CC0. Selected original OBJ oak, palm and rounded pine models, with their original material definitions. OBJ and MTL files are stored with a `.txt` extension. The original bundled license is `nature-LICENSE.txt`.
- [Quaternius Ultimate Nature Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatenature.html) was also researched. Its official page lists CC0 and FBX, OBJ and Blend formats. No assets from that pack were added here.

`DownhillBackdrop.cs` creates the terrain, planted terraces, skyline towers and placement locally. Imported house models are used only beyond the close street architecture. Tree material colors are adapted to the existing warm town palette. No raster foreground, road painting, downloaded scripts, purchases or account information are included.
